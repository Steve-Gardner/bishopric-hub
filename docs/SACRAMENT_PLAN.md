# Sacrament meeting: what to build

Source material: the Sacrament Meeting Speaker Tracker spreadsheet and the Sacrament Meeting Conducting Script.

## Loaded already
318 speaker rows from Jan 2021 to Feb 2026 in `bishopric.sacrament` (235 adult speakers, 38 youth speakers, 45 for people no longer in the ward directory, kept by name only). Topics and occasions (Mother's Day, Easter, Girls Camp, Father's Day) are in `details`. The spreadsheet's dates were a day early; they were moved to the Sunday.

## Build list (suggested order)
1. **Script wizard.** Pull the conductor, presider, speakers, prayers and hymns from the Sacrament tab and Bishopric Agenda; ask a few questions (baby blessing? releases or sustainings? stake business? musical number? visiting leaders?); fill in the script; show it on screen; later export a PDF (regular or dyslexic font for the bishop).
2. **Script template with switches.** The sections that appear or disappear: releases, sustainings, stake business, musical number, extra speaker slots, fast Sunday (testimony meeting replaces speakers), ward/stake conference, Primary Program, Christmas.
3. **Hymn and music fields.** Hymn number and title picker for opening, sacrament, closing and intermediate hymns, plus pianist, organist and chorister.
4. **Speaker priority list** (the spreadsheet dashboard). Age 12+, ranked by time since last spoke, colored: never, 2+ years, 1-2 years, within 1 year. The app already has a similar list; add the color bands, times spoken and the age column.
5. **Exclusions with a reason** (homebound, health, does not attend). Hide from the list; keep it across directory refreshes. Fits with the health notes in Individuals.
6. **Name matching** that knows nicknames (Steve/Steven, Bill/William) and families ("the Flammer family"), used when typing a speaker.
7. **Meeting types** (Regular, Fast Sunday, Ward Conference, Stake Conference, General Conference, High Council, Easter, Mother's Day, Father's Day, Girls Camp, Primary Program). Drive which script and which speaker slots show.
8. **Topic and group speaking** (for example "Sunday School presidency spoke about..."): one topic or theme per meeting, shown as the introduction line.
9. **Speaker history view** per person: every talk, date and topic. Also print a year-end summary.
10. **Directory sync** from the Church site (replaces the spreadsheet's PDF-paste roster import) with age updates and move-out flagging.
11. **Yearly planning grid:** the old yearly sheets listed the special Sundays and who conducts fast Sundays. Plan the year from the Default Monthly Schedule and Special Sacrament Meeting Dates.
